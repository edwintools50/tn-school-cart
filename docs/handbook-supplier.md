# Supplier (Seller) Handbook

Your guide to listing products and fulfilling orders on TN School Cart.

## 1. Creating your account

1. Go to **Sign up** and choose **Supplier**.
2. Fill in: your name, email, phone, password, **business name**, and **service area** (the district(s) you serve).
3. Submit. Your account status starts as **PENDING** — you can log in and prepare listings right away, but they won't be publicly visible until TN School Cart's admin team **approves your account**. This is a one-time check; approval usually doesn't take long. You'll be able to log in and use the dashboard while you wait.

## 2. Listing a product

1. From your **Supplier Dashboard**, click **+ Add product**.
2. Fill in: title, description, category, price, unit (piece/box/dozen/etc.), and stock quantity.
3. Add a product photo (optional, but listings with photos sell better).
4. Click **Submit for review**. Every new or edited listing goes to **PENDING** and needs admin approval before it appears in the public marketplace — this applies even after your account itself is approved, since each *listing* is checked separately.

### Selling a digital product (e-content)

If what you're selling is a downloadable file — a PDF guide, worksheet pack, chart set, etc. — instead of a physical item:

1. Tick **"This is a digital product (e-content)"** on the listing form. This hides the stock/unit fields (not needed — digital items never run out) and shows a file upload instead.
2. Upload your file: PDF, ZIP, DOC/DOCX, or PPT/PPTX, up to **3.5MB**.
3. Fill in the rest as normal (title, description, category, price) and submit.
4. Buyers who purchase a digital product get the download link by email automatically once they pay, plus it's always available on their order page. You don't need to do anything after the sale — delivery is automatic.

### Duplicating a listing

Selling similar items often? Click **Duplicate** next to any existing listing (on your dashboard) to start a new listing pre-filled with that one's title, description, category, price, unit/stock, photo, and digital file (if any). Just tweak what's different and submit — much faster than typing everything from scratch.

## 3. Editing or removing a listing

- Click **Edit** on any listing to change it. Saving an edit sends it back for admin review (status resets to `PENDING`) — this keeps the marketplace trustworthy, since changes are re-checked.
- Click **Delist** to pull a product off the marketplace without deleting it (you can't currently re-list a delisted item — create a fresh one, or duplicate it, if you want it back).
- If a listing is **REJECTED**, the reason will show on your dashboard under that product. Fix the issue and click **Edit** to resubmit.

## 4. Fulfilling orders

1. Click **Orders** on your dashboard to see everything buyers have ordered from you.
2. Each order item starts at `PLACED`. As you process it, update its status: `CONFIRMED → SHIPPED → DELIVERED` (or `CANCELLED` if you can't fulfill it).
3. Updating an item's status automatically sends the buyer a WhatsApp notification, so keep it current — it's their only visibility into progress besides checking the site themselves.
4. Digital-product orders don't need shipping status updates — delivery is automatic by email once paid, though the order line still shows in your Orders list for your records.

## 5. Getting paid

Payment is collected by TN School Cart via Razorpay at checkout — buyers pay online (UPI/card/netbanking/wallet), not cash on delivery. Payout/settlement to your business account is handled outside the app by TN School Cart's finance/admin process, not something you configure yourself here.

## 6. Account settings

- **Settings** (top nav) lets you change your password anytime.
- If you forget it, use **Forgot password?** on the login screen.

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Where |
|---|---|
| List a new product | Supplier Dashboard → + Add product |
| Sell a downloadable file | Tick "digital product" when listing, upload the file |
| List something similar to an existing item fast | Duplicate on that listing |
| Fix a rejected listing | Edit it, then resubmit |
| Update an order's shipping status | Orders → update item status |
| Stop selling something | Delist |
| Change my password | Settings |
