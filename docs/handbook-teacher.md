# Teacher Handbook

Your guide to finding teaching positions at Tamil Nadu schools through TN School Cart.

## 1. Creating your account

1. Go to **Sign up** and choose **Teacher**.
2. Fill in: your name, email, phone, password, **qualification** (e.g. "B.Ed with Tamil as a subject"), **subject specialization** (Tamil, English, Maths, Science, Social Science, Computer Science, Physical Education, Arts & Craft, Music, Primary Teacher, Special Educator, Librarian, or Other), **years of experience**, and **preferred district**.
3. Upload your **resume** (PDF, up to 5MB) — this gets attached automatically to every job you apply for, so you only upload it once.
4. Submit. Your account starts as **PENDING** until TN School Cart's admin team approves it. You can browse vacancies while waiting, but you won't be able to apply until approved.

## 2. Finding a job

1. Click **Job Vacancies** to browse open positions across Tamil Nadu.
2. Each listing shows the subject, employment type (full-time/part-time), school, district, qualification and experience required, and salary range (if the school shared one).
3. Open one you're interested in to read the full role description.

## 3. Applying

1. On the vacancy page, write a short **cover note** explaining why you're a good fit.
2. Submit. Your resume (from your profile) is attached automatically — you don't upload it again per application.
3. You can update your cover note any time before the school makes a decision.
4. Track your application status right there on the vacancy page: `PENDING`, `HIRED`, or `REJECTED`.

## 4. If you're hired

The school will contact you directly (they can see your profile, resume, and contact details once you've applied) to sort out start dates, paperwork, and everything else — TN School Cart's role ends at connecting you with the opportunity and tracking the application status; it doesn't manage the hiring process itself.

## 5. Keeping your profile current

- Go to your **Teacher Dashboard** to update your resume any time — useful if you've gained new qualifications or want to refresh it. The updated file applies to all *future* applications automatically.
- **Settings** (top nav) to change your password.
- **Forgot password?** on the login screen if you need a reset link emailed to you.

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Where |
|---|---|
| Find teaching jobs | Job Vacancies |
| Apply to one | Open it → write a cover note → submit |
| Check if I got the job | Job Vacancies page for that listing shows your status |
| Update my resume | Teacher Dashboard |
| Change my password | Settings |
