# Gig Worker Handbook

Your guide to finding paid campus jobs — plumbing, electrical, cleaning, IT support, catering, transport, and more — through TN School Cart.

## 1. Creating your account

1. Go to **Sign up** and choose **Gig Worker**.
2. Fill in: your name, email, phone, password, **business name** (or just your name if you work solo), and **service area** (district you cover).
3. Submit. Your account starts as **PENDING** until TN School Cart's admin team approves it. You can log in and set things up while you wait, but you can't submit offers or appear in the public directory until you're approved.

## 2. Two ways to get work

**A. List a standing Service** — a "shop window" that shows up in the **Find Services** directory, so principals browsing by category/district can see you exist and know your rates. This doesn't book a job by itself — a principal who's interested posts a matching gig request, which you then bid on (step B).

**B. Bid on open Gig Requests** — this is the actual booking mechanism. Principals post jobs, you submit a price + message, and if they accept, the job is yours.

Doing both — listing a service *and* actively browsing/bidding on gig requests — gets you the most visibility.

### Listing a service

1. From your **Worker Dashboard**, click **+ Add service**.
2. Fill in: category, title, description, pricing (fixed price, hourly rate, or "quote per job"), and your service area (district).
3. Submit — like all listings, it needs admin approval before it's publicly visible (status `PENDING` → `APPROVED`).
4. Use **Duplicate** on an existing service to quickly create a similar one instead of retyping everything.

### Bidding on a gig request

1. Click **Gig Requests** to browse open jobs (filter by category/district if you like).
2. Open one that matches what you do. Read the description, location, photo (if any), and budget/preferred date.
3. Submit an **offer**: your quoted price and a short message. You can update your offer any time before the principal accepts one (yours or someone else's).
4. If the principal accepts your offer, the request moves to `ASSIGNED` and all other offers on it are automatically rejected.

## 3. Doing the job

1. Once assigned, a **Chat on WhatsApp** button appears on the request page — use it to coordinate timing, access, and any details with the principal.
2. The principal marks the job **In progress** when work starts.
3. When you finish, upload a **completion photo** on the request page (this is your proof of work — do this before expecting final sign-off).
4. The principal reviews and marks the job **Completed**.
5. If something falls through, either side can **Cancel** the job at any point.

## 4. Getting paid

TN School Cart doesn't process payment for gig work — no online checkout for jobs, unlike marketplace product orders. You settle payment directly with the principal (cash, UPI, bank transfer — whatever you both agree) once the job is done, typically coordinated over the WhatsApp chat that opens after acceptance.

## 5. Account settings

- **Settings** (top nav) to change your password.
- **Forgot password?** on the login screen if you need a reset link emailed to you.

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Where |
|---|---|
| Show up in the directory for future work | Worker Dashboard → + Add service |
| Actually get booked for a job | Gig Requests → open one → submit an offer |
| Coordinate a job I've been assigned | Chat on WhatsApp on the request page |
| Prove I finished the job | Upload completion photo on the request page |
| List something similar to an existing service fast | Duplicate on that service |
| Change my password | Settings |
