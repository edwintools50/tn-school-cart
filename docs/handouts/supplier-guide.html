1 · Signing up & getting approved

  1. Go to tnschoolcart.com → Sign up → choose Supplier (Seller).
  2. Fill in your name, email, phone, a password, your business name, and the district you serve.
  3. Click Create account. You're logged in right away and land on your Supplier Dashboard.
About approval

Unlike principals, supplier accounts start as Pending and need a quick review by the TN School Cart admin team before your listings can go live. You don't have to wait idle — you can prepare your product listings right away; they just won't be reviewed until your account is approved.

2 · Listing your first product

From your dashboard, click + Add product and fill in:

FieldWhat to enter
TitleClear product name, e.g. "Ruled Notebook 200 Pages (Pack of 10)"
DescriptionWhat it is, materials, sizing — enough for a principal to order confidently without seeing it in person
CategoryStationery, Furniture, Educational Content, Notebooks & Books, Health & Hygiene, Sports, IT & Electronics, Exam & Print Stationery, Uniforms & Merchandise, or Other
UnitHow it's sold — piece, box, dozen, pack of 10, etc.
Price (₹)Price per unit
StockHow many units you currently have available
Image URL (optional)A link to a product photo hosted elsewhere

Click Submit for review. Your product won't appear in the marketplace until an admin approves it.

3 · Understanding listing status

Pending Approved Rejected Delisted

Your dashboard shows every product's status. If something is Rejected, the admin's reason is shown right there — fix the issue and edit the listing to resubmit it.

4 · Editing or delisting

  • Edit any listing to update price, stock, description, or category — saving sends it back to Pending for a quick re-check.
  • Delist a product any time to pull it off the marketplace immediately (e.g. you've sold out for the season).

5 · Fulfilling orders

Open Orders from your dashboard to see every order containing your products, with the school name, district, quantity, and price. Move each item forward as you fulfil it:

  1. Confirm order — you've seen it and will prepare it.
  2. Mark shipped — it's on its way.
  3. Mark delivered — the school has received it.

You can also Cancel an item while it's still in the "Placed" stage. A WhatsApp link sits next to every order item so you can message the school directly about delivery timing or details.

6 · Getting paid

Important

TN School Cart does not process real payments yet — the app automatically marks orders "paid" at checkout so you can start preparing them, but no money actually moves through the platform. You'll need to collect payment from the school directly (bank transfer, cash, etc.) exactly as you would offline, and use the WhatsApp link on the order to confirm payment received.

7 · Good-listing checklist

  • Keep stock counts accurate — schools can't order what shows as out of stock, and inaccurate stock leads to cancelled orders.
  • Update order status promptly so schools aren't left guessing.
  • Write titles the way a principal would search for the item, not internal product codes.
  • Double-check the category — a misfiled listing is harder for schools to find.

8 · Questions & answers

How long does approval take?
Usually 1–2 business days for your account, and similarly for each new listing.
Can I sell in more than one category?
Yes — list as many products across as many categories as you like from the same account.
What if my listing keeps getting rejected?
Check the rejection note on your dashboard, or message us on WhatsApp for specific feedback.
Can I change my service area later?
Message us on WhatsApp and we'll update it for you.
