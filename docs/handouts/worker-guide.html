1 · Signing up & getting approved

  1. Go to tnschoolcart.com → Sign up → choose Gig Worker.
  2. Fill in your name, email, phone, a password, your name or business name, and the district you work in.
  3. Click Create account. You're taken straight to your Worker Dashboard.
About approval

Worker accounts start as Pending and need a quick review by the admin team before your services go live — same as suppliers. You can prepare your listings while you wait.

3 · Understanding listing status

Pending Approved Rejected Delisted

Check your dashboard for status and, if rejected, the admin's reason — edit and resubmit once fixed.

4 · Finding jobs

There are two ways work reaches you:

A. Browse open requests

Go to Gig Requests in the top menu to see every open job posted by schools, filterable by category and district. This is the main way to find work actively.

B. Get found directly

Schools can also browse Find Services to see all approved worker listings by category — a strong, detailed listing means schools may reach out to you directly (via WhatsApp or by posting a matching request in your category).

5 · Sending an offer

  1. Open a job from Gig Requests that's still marked Open.
  2. Fill in your quote (₹) and a short message to the school.
  3. Click Submit offer. You can come back and update your quote or message any time while the job is still Open and your offer is Pending.

Track every offer you've ever sent — and its status — under My offers & jobs on your dashboard.

6 · Getting hired

When a school accepts your offer, the job moves to Assigned and your offer status becomes Accepted (any other offers on that job are automatically declined). You'll see this reflected in My offers & jobs.

7 · During the job

Good to know

Only the school can mark a job "In progress" or "Completed" — there's no button on your side for this. Message them on WhatsApp when you start and when you finish so they can update the status promptly.

8 · Getting paid

TN School Cart doesn't process payments between schools and workers — agree on price up front through your offer, and settle payment directly with the school once the job is done. Use the Chat on WhatsApp button that appears on the job page once you're assigned to confirm completion and payment.

9 · Questions & answers

Can I list more than one type of service?
Yes — add as many service listings as you like, across different categories.
Can I withdraw an offer?
Message the school directly on WhatsApp if you need to withdraw or renegotiate before they accept it.
What if I never hear back on an offer?
Schools can only accept one offer per job — if it goes to someone else, that job will show as Assigned and your offer as declined.
How do I change my service area?
Message us on WhatsApp and we'll update it for you.

Need help with your listings?

Message TN School Cart support directly on WhatsApp.
