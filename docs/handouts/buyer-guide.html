1 · Creating your account

No approval wait for principals — you're ready to order the moment you sign up.

  1. Go to tnschoolcart.com and click Sign up in the top right.
  2. Choose Principal (Buyer) from the three role buttons at the top of the form.
  3. Fill in your name, email, phone, a password, your school name, and your district.
  4. Click Create account. You're logged in immediately and taken straight to the marketplace — no waiting for anyone to approve you.

2 · Finding supplies

Everything suppliers have listed and TN School Cart's admin team has approved appears in the Marketplace.

  • Use the search box to look for a product by name, or the category dropdown to browse one section at a time — Stationery, Furniture, Notebooks & Books, Health & Hygiene, IT & Electronics, Exam & Print Stationery, Uniforms & Merchandise, and more.
  • Each product card shows the price, unit (per piece, per box, per pack, etc.), how many are in stock, and which supplier is selling it.
  • Click a product's title to see the full description and a bigger image before you order.

3 · Cart & checkout

  1. On any product, click Add to cart. You can adjust the quantity on the product page first, or change it later in your cart.
  2. Click Cart in the top menu to review everything — update quantities, remove items, and see your running total.
  3. Click Proceed to checkout. Confirm your school name, district, and type in the exact delivery address.
  4. Click Place order. Your order is created immediately and marked as paid.
About payment

Checkout on TN School Cart does not collect real money yet — placing an order marks it "paid" automatically so suppliers can start preparing it. Please settle the actual payment with the supplier directly (bank transfer, cash, etc.) as you would normally, and use the Chat on WhatsApp button on your order to confirm you've paid.

4 · Tracking your order

Open My Orders from the top menu any time to see every order you've placed and its status. Click into an order to see item-by-item progress:

Placed Confirmed Shipped Delivered Cancelled

Each item moves through these stages as the supplier updates it on their end. If an order has multiple products from different suppliers, each one is tracked separately.

5 · Requesting a service job

Need a plumber, electrician, cleaning crew, painter, caterer, transport, or anything else campus-related? Post a gig request and workers in your area will send you offers.

  1. Go to Gig Requests or My Gig Requests in the top menu and click + Post a gig request.
    Tip: browsing Find Services first and clicking "Post a gig request for this" on a worker's listing pre-fills the right category for you.
  2. Pick the type of work (plumbing, electrical, cleaning, painting/whitewashing, carpentry, IT/printer support, RO water purifier service, smart board/CCTV/broadband installation, printing services, catering, transport, and more).
  3. Give it a clear job title and describe the work needed.
  4. Confirm the school name, district and address, and optionally add a preferred date and budget.
  5. Click Post gig request.

6 · Choosing a worker

Once your request is posted, gig workers in that category can send you an offer — their quoted price and a short message. Open your gig request any time to see every offer that's come in.

  1. Compare the quoted prices, messages, and each worker's listed service area.
  2. Click Accept this offer on the one you want. The job is now assigned to that worker, and any other offers are automatically declined.

7 · Managing the job

Once a worker is assigned, only you (the school) can move the job forward — the worker doesn't have a button for this, so keep it updated as work happens:

  • Mark in progress — once the worker has started.
  • Mark completed — once the job is finished, so you can confirm and close it out.
  • Cancel job — available at any point if plans change.

8 · Getting help fast

A green WhatsApp button sits in the bottom-right corner of every page — click it any time to message TN School Cart support directly. On order and gig-request pages, a Chat on WhatsApp button also appears with the order or job details already typed in for you, so confirming, coordinating, or reporting an issue takes one tap.

9 · Questions & answers

I forgot my password. What do I do?
There's no self-service reset yet — message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you regain access.
Can I cancel an order after placing it?
Once an item is Confirmed or later, ask the supplier directly via WhatsApp. While it's still "Placed," the supplier can cancel it from their side.
I can't find a category I need.
Message us on WhatsApp — new categories get added as more suppliers and workers join.
Do I pay through the website?
Not yet. See the payment note in section 3 — settle payment directly with the supplier or worker and confirm via WhatsApp.
