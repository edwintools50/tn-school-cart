1 · What admin is responsible for

Admin's job is moderation and oversight, not running individual orders or jobs — those stay between the principal, the supplier, and the worker. Specifically, admin:

  • Approves or rejects new supplier and gig worker accounts (principals are auto-approved and never need review).
  • Approves or rejects every new or edited product and service listing before it becomes publicly visible.
  • Can suspend an account that's misbehaving, and reinstate one later.
  • Has a read-only view of every order and gig request placed on the platform, for spotting problems.

Click any card to jump straight to the filtered list. A tab bar (Dashboard / Users / Products / Services / Orders / Gigs) sits at the top of every admin page for quick navigation.

3 · Reviewing accounts

Go to Users and filter by status or role. For each Pending supplier or worker, check before deciding:

  • Does the business name and service area look genuine and specific (not obviously fake or spam)?
  • Is the phone number and email plausible?
  • Does what they're likely to sell/offer make sense for a school marketplace?

Note: there's no automated identity or document verification — this is a manual judgment call based on what's visible.

Approve
Looks legitimate — unlocks their dashboard and listing review
Reject (+ reason)
Doesn't look genuine — they'll see your reason on their account
Suspend
An approved account starts misbehaving
Reinstate
A rejected/suspended account should be let back in

4 · Reviewing listings

Go to Products or Services and filter to Pending. For each one, check:

  • Is the category correct for what's being sold/offered?
  • Is the price reasonable — not zero, not absurdly high?
  • Is the description clear enough for a principal to order confidently?
  • Does it look like spam or an exact duplicate of another listing?

Click Approve, or Reject with a short reason so the supplier/worker knows exactly what to fix and can resubmit.

5 · Orders & gigs oversight

Orders and Gigs in the admin tabs are read-only — you can't edit them, but they're useful for spotting things that need a nudge: an order stuck on "Placed" for a long time, or a gig request that's been "Open" for weeks with no offers.

6 · Disputes & escalation

Good to know

There's no in-app messaging or dispute-resolution tool yet. If a school or vendor reports a problem, admin's options are limited to suspending the offending account or rejecting a listing — anything beyond that means reaching out directly by phone or WhatsApp using the contact details on file for that account.

7 · Adding a new category

New product or gig-work categories aren't self-service from the admin panel — they require a small code change (see the Database Maintenance Handbook, "Adding a new marketplace category"). If a supplier or worker requests one that doesn't fit anywhere, note it and pass it to whoever maintains the site's code.

8 · Suggested rhythm

  • Check the Users and Products/Services pending queues at least every 1–2 days, so suppliers and workers aren't left waiting to start selling.
  • Skim Orders and Gigs weekly for anything that looks stuck.
