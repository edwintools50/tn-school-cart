# Principal / Head Master Handbook

Your guide to buying supplies, hiring gig workers, and posting teacher vacancies on TN School Cart.

## 1. Creating your account

1. Go to **Sign up** and choose **Principal / Head Master**.
2. Fill in: your name, email, phone, password, **school name**, **UDISE number** (11 digits), and **district**.
3. Upload a **school verification photo** (a photo of the school signboard/building — helps the admin team confirm you're a real school).
4. Submit. Your account is **approved instantly** — no waiting. You're logged straight into the marketplace.

You do not need admin approval to start browsing or buying. (Suppliers, gig workers, and teachers do need approval — that's normal on their side, not yours.)

## 2. Buying supplies from the Marketplace

1. Click **Marketplace** in the top nav.
2. Filter by **category** (Stationery, Furniture, Educational Content, IT/Electronics, Uniforms, etc.) or search by keyword.
3. Each product card shows the price, unit, and stock. A **"Digital"** badge means it's an e-content item (PDF/guide/worksheet) delivered by download, not a physical shipment.
4. Click **Add to cart** on as many items as you need, from as many suppliers as you like — one cart, one checkout, even across suppliers.
5. Open **Cart** (top nav) to review quantities, remove items, or adjust quantities before checking out.

## 3. Checkout & payment

1. Click **Proceed to checkout** from your cart.
2. **If your cart has only digital items**, you'll skip straight to payment — no delivery address needed, since nothing ships physically.
3. **If your cart has any physical items**, fill in delivery details: school name, UDISE, district, taluk, block, pin code, and full address. These pre-fill from your profile where possible.
4. Click **Place order**. You'll be taken to a secure Razorpay payment screen — pay by UPI, card, netbanking, or wallet.
5. After payment, you land on your **Order** page showing what you bought and the payment status.

**For digital items:** once payment is confirmed, you'll get an email with the download link(s), and the same link also appears permanently on your Order page under each digital item — so if you ever lose the email, just come back to **My Orders**.

## 4. Tracking your orders

- **My Orders** (top nav) lists every order you've placed, newest first.
- Open any order to see per-item status: `PLACED → CONFIRMED → SHIPPED → DELIVERED` (physical items) or `CANCELLED` if a supplier can't fulfill it.
- Each order page has a **Chat on WhatsApp** button that opens a pre-filled message to TN School Cart support referencing that exact order — use it if something's stuck or wrong.

## 5. Hiring a gig worker (plumbing, electrical, cleaning, IT support, etc.)

TN School Cart doesn't let you "buy" a service directly — you post what you need and workers bid on it.

1. (Optional) Browse **Find Services** to see what gig workers are already offering in your district, by category — useful for gauging typical pricing before you post.
2. Click **Gig Requests → Post a gig request** (or "Post a gig request for this" from a service card, which pre-fills the category).
3. Fill in: category, job title, description, school name, UDISE, district/taluk/block/pin code, address, an optional **photo of the issue**, and an optional preferred date and budget.
4. Submit. Your request appears under **My Gig Requests**, status `OPEN`.
5. Gig workers in your area submit **offers** (a quoted price + message). Open the request to see all offers as they arrive.
6. Click **Accept this offer** on the one you want. This automatically rejects the other offers and moves the request to `ASSIGNED`.
7. A **Chat on WhatsApp** button appears once someone's assigned — use it to coordinate timing and access.
8. As work progresses, use the buttons on the request page to mark it **In progress**, then the worker uploads a **completion photo** when done, and you mark it **Completed** (or **Cancel** at any point if needed).

**Payment for gig work happens outside the app** — TN School Cart only handles discovery, bidding, and status tracking, not the money. Settle payment directly with the worker (cash, UPI, etc.) once the job's done, coordinated over WhatsApp.

## 6. Hiring a teacher

1. Click **Job Vacancies → Post a vacancy**.
2. Fill in: subject, job title, description, school details (same fields as a gig request), employment type (full-time/part-time), optional salary range, qualification required, and experience required.
3. Submit — it appears under **My Job Vacancies**, status `OPEN`.
4. Teachers apply with a cover note (their resume is automatically attached from their profile). Open the vacancy to see all applicants, their qualification, subject specialization, years of experience, and a link to their resume.
5. Click **Hire this teacher** on the one you choose. You can then contact them directly (their profile has phone/email) to finalize things — the platform doesn't manage the hiring paperwork itself, just discovery and applications.
6. Click **Close vacancy** once you're done, so it stops accepting applications.

## 7. Account settings

- **Settings** (top nav) lets you change your password anytime — enter your current password, then your new one twice.
- If you forget your password, use **Forgot password?** on the login page — a reset link is emailed to you (expires in 1 hour).
